# Level layout contract (Lead owns; generator builds to this)

All numbers are in pixels unless noted. Tile = 16px.

## Size (docs/PRODUCT.md, Oct 25 evening)
- Every stage has **3-4 floors**; stage 1 is 3 floors and **~3 screens wide** (232-248 tiles = 3712-3968px; the screen is 1280x720 at zoom 1). Stages 2-5 grow from there (table below).
- The **exit is always on a different floor from the start** (start: ground floor at one end; exit: top floor at the other end).

## Floors
- Each floor band is 10 tiles (160px) tall.
- The bottom tile row of each band is the floor slab: solid StaticBody2D / TileMap collision on physics layer 1 (`world`).
- Ceiling: the slab of the floor above. The top floor gets its own 1-tile ceiling.
- Floor N's walkable surface y = level_origin_y - N * 160 (N = 0 is ground floor).

## Movement constants (from PlayerStats)
- gravity 980 px/s^2 (project setting `physics/2d/default_gravity`).
- jump_velocity 320 -> apex ~52px (3.25 tiles). Clears a 2-tile (32px) prop, never a puppet, never a floor.

## Rooms
- A room is **24-40 tiles** wide (6-10 four-tile units), one floor tall, so a 21-tile stair footprint always fits inside one room. Room walls **stack floor to floor** (one partition per level), so a room on floor f and the room above it share their walls and a stair fits in both. Rooms on the same floor join through a doorway 3 tiles high in the shared wall (doorways and solid walls are rolled per floor).
- Room kits from Designer: width multiple of 4 tiles, height exactly 10 tiles, floor slab included, doorway gaps at x=0 and x=width on rows 6-8 (counted from top, 0-based).

## Stairs (final numbers, Lead decision Oct 25)
- The only way between floors. A **diagonal staircase**: **15 tiles (240px) horizontal run** rising **one floor (160px)**, ~34 deg (matches the `boy_climb_stairs` art), plus a **flat 3-tile (48px) landing** at the bottom and at the top. **Total footprint 21 tiles (336px).**
- Data: `{from_floor, to_floor, bottom, top}`, `to_floor = from_floor + 1`. **`bottom`** = start of the diagonal at the end of the bottom landing (on `from_floor`'s surface); **`top`** = end of the diagonal at the start of the top landing (on `to_floor`'s surface). `|top.x - bottom.x| = 240` exactly; the stair rises left or right. The bottom landing lies on the far side of `bottom` from the run, the top landing beyond `top`. Helpers: `LevelValidator.stair_span(s)` (footprint `[x0, x1]`), `LevelValidator.stair_landings(s)`; constants `STAIR_RUN_PX 240`, `STAIR_LANDING_PX 48`, `STAIR_FOOTPRINT_PX 336`.
- **Footprint kept clear on both floors:** no wall column (doorways included), furniture, other stairs, props, gold, chests, enemy spawns, start or exit anywhere in the 21 tiles, so the landings are always clear and a puppet can't spawn on the top step.
- Node: `Stair` (`scripts/world/stair.gd`, BE) `Area2D` in group `stairs`, a segment from `bottom` to `top`; the player presses W/S (up/down) on it to climb (`boy_climb_stairs`), jump is disabled while climbing.
- **Placement (docs/DESIGN.md, Designer #20):** a stair is placed by its **leftmost footprint tile `x`** (footprint = tiles `x .. x+20`). Rising right: `bottom` = left edge of tile **x+3** on the lower floor, `top` = left edge of tile **x+18** on the upper floor. Rising left mirrors the columns: `bottom` = x+18, `top` = x+3. Both ends sit on tile left edges (validator rule). `LevelValidator.stair_x(s)` gives `x`.
- **Slab cutout:** the upper floor's slab is cut open over the 9 diagonal tiles next to `top`: tiles **x+9..x+17** rising right, **x+3..x+11** rising left (`LevelValidator.stair_hole(s)`, 144px, `STAIR_HOLE_PX`). The hole has no collision (the boy climbs up through it) and is a 144px gap: not walkable and not jumpable, so it is a **blocker in the floor graph**. It splits the upper floor; the stair's top attaches to the landing side. The slab end tile goes next to it (`slab_end_right` at x+8 / `slab_end_left` at x+12).
- **Cutout rules (validator):** nothing (start, exit, props, gold, chests, enemy spawns) may stand over the hole on the upper floor, and since the hole is a graph blocker, the reachability rule (exit, gold, chests) can never rely on floor the cutout removes. The generator places stairs flush against walls/other stairs where possible, refuses a stair whose cutout would strand the exit or another stair, and accepts a repair stair only if it grows the reachable floor. Short floor left between a cutout and a wall ("pockets") is allowed to stay unreached and is never given gold, chests or the exit.
- **Gallery rail (PRODUCT.md "Stair opening", QA-revised Oct 25 8:30 PM):**
  - *Visual:* `stair_gallery_rail` (Designer #33, atlas `[9, 2, 6, 3]`, `TilesStairs`) is painted across the whole opening (x+9..x+14, with `stair_rail_cap` over x+15..x+17; mirrored for left-rising stairs). If the manifest lacks it, `stair_rail_cap` is repeated over the opening instead.
  - *Collision:* a `Rails` `StaticBody2D` on world layer 1 (`LAYER_WORLD`, mask 0) holds two thin 6px x 96px walls per stair, both standing on the upper floor's surface. 96px (`RAIL_H_PX`) clears the boy's jump apex (~52-55px) with room for future jump boosts; a test requires it to exceed the apex at the `PlayerStats` stat cap by >= 16px. The visible handrail is 28px and the finial 40px, so the rest is invisible.
    - *Far-edge wall (`LevelValidator.stair_rail`):* where the upper floor drops off, on the solid slab. Rising right, it's px [138, 144) from the stair's leftmost edge, ending at the left edge of x+9. Rising left, it's mirrored at px [192, 198).
    - *Near-edge guard (`stair_rail_near`):* inside the opening, 16px back from the top landing. Rising right, it's px [266, 272); rising left, [64, 70). This guard is not in the QA spec, which asked for the far edge only. It was added because the physics test proved that a body walking off the top landing back toward the opening dropped a floor (stairs are an `Area2D`, so they don't collide). The 16px set-back is bigger than the widest body radius (14px), so a body standing on the stair's top point never touches the guard.
  - The boy and walking enemies mask WORLD, so they are stopped. Floaters (stages 4-5) don't mask WORLD and drift through. No mask is toggled anywhere.
  - Climbing is kinematic (no `move_and_slide` while climbing, for both the boy and enemies), so climbers never collide with either wall. Where a climber passes the far wall's x, it is about 96px under the wall's foot. Jump is disabled while climbing, so a jump on the stair can't bump the slab or the post.
  - *Entry zone (QA B2, `scripts/levelgen/level_stair.gd`, `class_name LevelStair extends Stair`):* the generator builds every stair as a `LevelStair`. BE's `Stair` trigger is the whole run box (bottom-x..top-x, a full floor band above), so on the upper floor it covered the gallery slab behind the far wall: pressing down there entered CLIMB and the approach slid the boy across the opening (through the rail line) to the top. `LevelStair.contains` (BE's `is_near` calls it) keeps BE's behaviour on the lower floor and on the stair line, but on the upper floor it only accepts the top-landing side (`upper_entry_ok`: at or beyond `top.x` toward the landing) plus the landing itself (48px, `LANDING_REACH`), so the boy enters going down only from the top landing and never through the rails. `player_controller.gd` and `stair.gd` (BE) are unchanged; enemies don't use `contains` (they use `LevelNav` + `Stair.trigger_rect`).
  - The validator rejects a far wall that is not touching the opening's far edge on slab inside the footprint, or a near guard that is not inside the opening 16px back. The 2px head-brush headroom change was dropped per steering.
- **Art (`LevelSkin`, default painter):** builds `TileMapLayer`s from `tiles_stage1` (21x15 atlas, indexes 0-23 unchanged): `TilesBack` (z -15: ceiling trim, wallpaper per room, wainscot), `TilesStairs` (z -6: `stair_stamp` 15x10, `landing_bottom` 3x3, `landing_top` 3x3, `stair_rail_cap` 3x2, flipped horizontally for left-rising stairs) and `TilesFront` (z -5: `floor_top` slabs with the cutout left open, slab ends, wall columns, door lintels and posts). Atlas row 5 lines up with the upper slab row. When it paints, the placeholder slab/wall colours and stair lines are hidden; if the PNG is missing the placeholders stay. `LevelGenerator.skin` (Callable `(level, layout)`) replaces it. Visual only: collision stays the generator's (slabs split around each hole).

## Enemies
- Default movement is `walk`: gravity, floor collision, and they change floors only via stairs (same floor graph as the player).
- `EnemyDef.movement = &"float"` enemies (**stages 4-5 only**) ignore gravity and **drift through floor slabs (not blocked by slabs)**, matching BE PR #26.
- Stage table (`scripts/levelgen/stage_table.gd`): stages 1-3 roll only `walk` types; the `float` tier (t4/t5) is allowed only at stages 4-5 (`ENEMY_TYPES[id].min_stage`, enforced by the generator and tested).
- **Every spawned enemy is a real EnemyDef-based enemy** (`scripts/levelgen/enemy_def.gd`, `class_name EnemyDef`: `type_id`, `movement`, `min_stage`, `script_path`). `StageTable.enemy_def(id)` builds it from `ENEMY_TYPES`; `build()` instances the def's script (BE's `Enemy` / `puppet_hand.gd`), sets its exported `def` (BE `apply_def` copies the fields in `_ready`) and meta `enemy_def`. The count is exactly the table's - no stand-in fill. t2-t5 currently reuse the puppet_hand body script, driven by their own EnemyDef (type_id/movement) until BE ships their scenes.
- **Spawn shape (QA B1):** each enemy gets BE's shape as BE's scenes make it, a circle r14 centred on the node origin (`_circle_centred`), never offset. The node is placed at layout feet point minus BE's `_foot()` (feet on the floor). The generator does not touch the Hitbox.
- **Walkers are reachable (route-gap fix):** walking enemies only spawn on floor-graph segments reachable from the start (`LevelValidator` rule `walker ... not reachable from the start`); before this, a walker could spawn in a gallery sealed between the far sides of two openings and could never move (QA: stage 5 seed 11). Floaters may spawn anywhere.
- **Low furniture (`LowFurniture` body):** furniture <= `MAX_JUMP_TILES` (2) tall is its own StaticBody2D (layer WORLD) with a collision exception for every enemy: walkers can't jump, and the floor graph already treats low furniture as walkable, so a walker parked against a 1-2 tile chest of drawers forever. The boy still collides with it (jumps it). A **climb-over cluster** is the boy's perch: a 3-tile piece with a **1-2 tile step piece touching it on each side** (steps are ordinary furniture, 2-3 tiles wide) and 2 tiles of open floor (`MOUNT_RUN_TILES`) beyond both steps (`LevelValidator.mount_passable`, `step_pieces`). Every single climb is then <= 32px (`max_single_climb_px`: floor -> 32px step, step -> 48px top is 16px) against a worst-case apex of 52.24px (`min_jump_apex`), a **20.2px margin** (`climb_margin_px`, must be >= `MOUNT_MARGIN_PX` 16) instead of the 4.2px of a bare 48px piece. It does not split the floor graph, so it gets the same walker exception (apex 52px; `can_mount(h)` is the kinematic bound: 32px yes, 48px no). Furniture taller than that (4 tiles), a 3-tile piece without a step on both sides, or one boxed in closer than 2 tiles, stays in `Geometry` and blocks both (a graph blocker; the repair pass lowers it if it strands anything). Every floor has at least one reachable 3-tile piece (`LevelValidator.mountable_floor_errors`, a generator guarantee).
- Back End owns enemy movement/pathing. The generator only supplies data: each spawned enemy gets meta `enemy_type`, `movement`, `home` (and the `movement` property when the script has one), and the floor graph below.

## Generator guarantees (validator rejects + rerolls with derived sub-seeds; last resort after 20 tries)
- **Matches the stage table** (validator, unless built with custom `opts.params`): floor count, width range, budget equal to `StageTable.budget(stage)` (counts come from the table, never scaled by width), enemy types in `ENEMY_TYPES`, never before their `min_stage`, movement equal to the table. Safe radius is `max(layout, table, 1280)`.
- 3-4 floors; exit on a different floor from the start.
- Exit reachable from start by walking + stairs only (no forced jump over anything taller than 2 tiles, no forced pickup/throw). Every gold pile, chest and walking enemy is reachable.
- **No enemy within one screen (1280px straight-line distance, any floor) of the start, none inside the start camera view** (main.gd's clamped camera, `LevelValidator.start_view`), and none in line of sight of the start (same floor, within 1280px, no solid wall or blocking (>3-tile / boxed-in) furniture between). Tension = sneaking toward something unseen.
- **No prop ever has to be broken:** every floor prop has a clear jump lane of 64px each side (no walls or doorway lintels, furniture, stair footprints, other props, chests, start or exit), and a plain jump at PlayerStats speed/jump/gravity clears its touch radius (`LevelValidator.prop_jump_clears`, tested). So there is always a route to the exit that breaks nothing.
- **No overlaps:** start, exit, stair footprints, props, gold, chests and enemies each own a footprint on their floor and none overlap; nothing is inside a wall column or furniture; everything is inside the level bounds.
- **Full content budget** (table above).
- **Deterministic:** same seed + stage = the same layout, and `build()` makes an identical node tree (names, positions, hidden vase gold).
- **Budgets are hard:** an under-budget attempt is never returned; it is rejected and the next derived sub-seed is tried.
- **Stairs stay clear:** the whole 21-tile footprint (both landings + run) is reserved on both floors - no props at the bottom, no enemies on the top steps, no chests/gold on landings, nothing over the slab cutout.
- **Last resort (PD):** if all 20 derived sub-seeds fail, `fallback_layout(stage)` returns that stage's known-good layout (one per stage, built to the stage's table: floors, width, budget, 1280px safe radius, clear stairs, real EnemyDefs; all 5 validated by `fallbacks_match_stage_table_and_are_only_last_resort`). Swapping it in is never silent: `push_warning("LevelGenerator: seed S stage N failed all 20 attempts ...")`, and `layout.fallback = true`. The seed sweep / CI counts any fallback use as a **failure** and must report 0 fallbacks.

## Generator (`scripts/levelgen/`)
- `LevelGenerator.generate(seed, stage, opts = {}) -> Dictionary` - pure data. Attempt *k* uses `RandomNumberGenerator` seeded with `hash("qm:<seed>:<stage>:<k>")`; up to 20 attempts, then `fallback_layout(stage)` (hand-made, validated in tests). The result records `attempt`, `rerolls`, `fallback`, `reject_reasons`. `opts`: `params` (override stage-table keys), `max_attempts`.
- The level is partitioned into rooms of 24-40 tiles (4-tile multiples), the same walls on every floor. Interior walls have a doorway unless `solid_wall_chance` rolls; 2-3 stairs are placed per floor pair, then a repair pass adds stairs (or, when no stair fits, opens a solid wall into a doorway / lowers blocking furniture to 2 tiles) until every walkable segment is reachable from the start. Furniture is placed AFTER that connect pass: a blocking piece (4 tiles, or a boxed-in 3) is kept only where it leaves every segment reachable (flush against a solid wall, or both sides still joined), then one climb-over cluster (step, 3-tile piece, step; `_place_cluster`) is guaranteed per floor. (Before, ~90% of rolled tall pieces were lowered to 2 tiles by the repair pass.) Then props (with jump lanes), gold, chests and enemies are placed into free floor space.
- `LevelGenerator.build(layout, world) -> {level, player, exit, enemies, stairs, bounds, nav, layout}` makes nodes under `world` (main.tscn's `World`, using its Player/Exit/Puppets/Obstacles holders). Invalid layouts build the fallback; a null world returns `{}`. Calls `seed(build_seed)` first so vase gold is deterministic too.
- The boy uses a feet origin (circle r12 at (0, -12)), like the bottom-centre art pivot. Enemies use BE's centred circle (r14 on the origin); feet = origin + `_foot()`.
- `LevelValidator.validate(layout) -> PackedStringArray` (empty = OK). Never crashes on null/garbage.
- `main.gd` exposes `level_seed: int` (-1 = roll a new random seed) and `stage: int`. FE (`FESignals`) sets both on the new instance for Retry seed / New run / next stage. `-- --seed=N [--stage=S]` (or `--seed N`) and web `?seed=N&stage=S` apply on the **first boot only** and never override values FE set on a reload. Level completion = `GameState.reached_exit` (no extra signal). The seed shows under the hint line.

### Layout data (feet points in World space; floor f surface y = -f*160)
`floors, width_tiles, rooms[{floor,x0,x1}], walls[{floor,x,doorway}], furniture[{floor,x,w,h}]` (tiles), `stairs[{from_floor,to_floor,bottom,top}], props[{type,pos}], gold[{pos,amount}], chests[{pos}], enemies[{type,movement,pos,home,floor}], start, exit, budget{enemies,props,gold,chests}, safe_radius`, plus `seed, stage, attempt, rerolls, fallback, reject_reasons, build_seed`.

## Floor graph / stair data for enemies (`LevelNav`)
Plain data, no nodes, no rendering: works the same for off-screen enemies.
- `LevelNav.current` - the loaded level's nav (null with no generated level). Also on the Level node as meta `nav`.
- `nav.stairs` - `[{from_floor, to_floor, bottom, top}]`, world-space feet points (bottom on `from_floor`, top on `to_floor`).
- `nav.graph` - `{segments: {floor: [Vector2(x_from, x_to)]}, adj: {"f:i": [{to: "g:j", stair: index}]}}`. A segment is a walkable stretch of one floor between solid walls / blocking furniture (4 tiles, or a boxed-in 3); edges are stairs. `LevelNav.nodes_for(p)` maps a point inside a blocker (e.g. the boy perched on a 4-tile piece's span) to the segment on EACH side; `route` takes the shorter, so a walker on either side walks straight to it.
- `nav.floor_of(pos)`, `nav.node_for(pos)` (`"f:i"`; nearest segment if pos is inside a blocker).
- `nav.route(a, b)` - `[]` same segment, `null` unreachable, else `[{stair, enter, exit}]` hops in order.
- `nav.next_waypoint(a, b)` - `{}` unreachable, `{point}` walk straight to it, or `{point, stair_to, stair}` walk to `point`, climb to `stair_to`. Whenever b's floor differs from a's, the answer routes via a stair (it never returns `{}` while a stair route exists). Callers re-plan every frame (BE's chase calls it each tick), so a target that changes floor is re-planned at once.
- **Targets on a stair (route-gap fix):** `nav.stair_at(p)` is the stair whose line is within `ON_STAIR_PX` (20px) of p, strictly between its landings. If b is on stair `si` (the boy mid-climb) and a isn't, `next_waypoint` heads for that stair's nearer landing by route length (`_toward_stair`) and climbs it, instead of mapping b to the floor below/above (walkers used to park directly under or over a climbing boy).

## Physics climb test (worst stats + a vase)

`kinematic can_mount` is only the generator's filter; the claim "the guaranteed piece is climbable at the worst stats any item combo allows" is checked by the real player scene (`tests/climb_sim.gd`, `class_name ClimbSim`). Each **hop** teleports the boy to stand still against a wall, presses move + jump once (no run-up), lets go of the direction once he is 6px over the near edge, and must land standing on the target top. A cluster climb is floor -> step -> 3-tall top -> walk off over the far step to the floor, in both directions. Setups (`ClimbSim.SETUPS`): `base` (140 px/s), `carry0.7` (Heavy Vases x3, carry slow 0.7 cap, vase in hand, 98 px/s) and `worst` (move_speed stat at stat_min 0.5, + the same vase carry: 70 px/s, the product is floored at 0.5x). Jump velocity and gravity are pinned (no item changes them; `jump_velocity` at `stat_min`).

- `tests/scene_climb.gd` (in `run_tests.gd`): a sample plus flat-world calibration (32px step climbed under every setup, a 60px box not).
- `tests/climb_sweep.gd`: `godot --headless --fixed-fps 60 -s res://tests/climb_sweep.gd -- seeds=50` (50 seeds x 5 stages x 3 setups, every floor's cluster, both directions; exits 1 on a failed climb). Needs `--fixed-fps 60` and `Engine.time_scale = 1` (physics delta scales with time_scale).
- `tests/levelgen_sweep.gd` also checks every guaranteed piece has both steps and the margin, and runs `SWEEP_CLIMB` (20) seeds per stage of the physics climb.
- Flat-world measurement of the standstill-climbable height of a free box: 50.5px at base, 53px with the 0.7 carry slow, 54px at the worst setup (slower is not harder: the circle gets more time over the corner). The cluster keeps every climb to 32px.
